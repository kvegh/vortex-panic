# Decision v2 — 2026-07-26

## Changes from v1

1. **Speed display**: show all three units simultaneously — fraction of c, km/s, and km/h. Humans can relate to km/h at low speeds, km/s becomes meaningful at orbital speeds, fraction of c at relativistic speeds.

2. **Ship direction**: ship flies from right to left (not left to right). Destinations are to the left, Earth recedes to the right.

3. **Distance from Earth**: always displayed, in both km and light-years simultaneously.

4. **Estimated time to target**: show how long it will take to reach the selected destination at current velocity/acceleration.

5. **Time dilation for round trip**: display how much more time will pass on Earth assuming a symmetrical flight (same accel/decel profile there and back). This is the "you leave, you come back, your twin is older" number.
