# VORTEX PANIC

**Vastly Overambitious Relativistic Travel EXperience with Proportional AstroNavigation Illustrating Cosmic-insignificance**

*A browser-based 2D space travel simulator inspired by the Total Perspective Vortex.*

## What is this?

A single-file HTML/Canvas simulation that lets you pilot a spacecraft from the Moon to the edge of the observable universe — and feel every lightyear of it.

## Physics

- **Newtonian gravity** — N-body gravitational attraction between objects
- **Gravity slingshots** — use planetary bodies to gain velocity for free
- **Relativistic velocity addition** — two objects can't exceed c, even when accelerating in opposite directions
- **Time dilation** — proper time on the ship diverges from coordinate time as you approach c
- **Rocket equation / fuel mass problem** — propellant has mass; accelerating more fuel costs more fuel
- **Acceleration & deceleration** — you must slow down before arrival, doubling your fuel problem
- **Human g-force limits** — crew can't survive arbitrary acceleration; ~1g sustained is the practical ceiling
